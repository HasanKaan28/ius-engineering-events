P: Please choose the type of your Student Club:
P: AEC (Academic and Educational Club): provides attention, support and recognition to all IUS students toward the high standards of the academic mission at IUS.
P: CSC (Community Service Club): builds community and creates connections among students at IUS.
P: SRC (Spiritual and Religious Club): faith-based societies formed by students who want to stay in touch with students of the same background.
P: CC (Cultural Club): student groups that exist to network with people coming from the same parts of the world, same background, and share a similar culture. The aim is to represent their tradition to other people.
P: RSC (Recreation and Sports Club): activities are organized so students can stay fit, healthy and spend time on their hobbies, sports competitions or in nature.
P: MAC (Media and Art Club): offer a variety of interesting activities, projects and content for students who would like to exercise and improve their creative skills.
