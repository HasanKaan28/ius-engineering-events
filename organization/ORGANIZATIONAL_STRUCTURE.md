* **Vice President & Operations Co-Leader (Mahmut İhsan Avcı)**: [ASIL BAŞKAN YARDIMCISI] Direct right hand to the President, manages day-to-day team syncs, member intake, and administrative/logistical execution.

* **PR & Media Lead (Bakir Bašić)**: [YÖNETİM KURULU ÜYESİ] Manages branding, social media accounts, visual identity, event flyers, and campus promotion.

### 2. Software & AI Committee
* **Focus**: Full-Stack Development, Modern Cloud, LLM & AI Agents, Data Engineering, Competitive Programming.
* **Key Formats**: Hands-on code-alongs, 48-hour hackathons, tech talks from senior software engineers.

### 3. Multidisciplinary Engineering Committee
* **Focus**: Electrical & Electronics, IoT, Embedded Systems, Mechanical CAD/3D Printing, Industrial automation.
* **Key Formats**: Hardware hackathons, Arduino/ESP32 & STM32 workshops, lab showcases in FENS maker spaces.

### 4. Applied Engineering Projects & Prototypes
* **Focus**: Multidisciplinary student project circles, embedded hardware prototypes, software utilities, and semester demo showcases.
* **Key Formats**: Project group formation, lab prototyping, technical mentoring, and demo day presentations.

### 5. Event Operations & Logistics
* **Focus**: Stage and venue management, sound/AV testing, guest speaker hospitality, badge distribution, and attendee flow control.

### 6. PR, Branding & Corporate Partnerships
* **Focus**: High-fidelity visual identity (Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube), promotional teaser reels, sponsor acquisition decks, and company relations.

### 7. Social Life & Fun Engineering Branch
* **Lead**: Muhammed Efe Ural (FENS 2nd Year)
* **Focus**: Campus social vibe, community cohesion, gaming tournaments (FIFA, CS2, board games), pizza & coding nights, and lighthearted engineering challenges (spaghetti bridge, egg-drop, robot-sumo).
* **Key Formats**: Monthly gaming cups, weekend team dinners, semester social kickoffs.
