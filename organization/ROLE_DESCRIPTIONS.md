# Role Descriptions & Operational Responsibilities

## 1. Executive Club President & Founder (Asıl Kulüp Başkanı) — Hasan Kaan
* **Core Mission**: Set the club's comprehensive vision, architect all technical, logistical, and operational workflows, and act as the ultimate decision-maker across all committees.
* **Direct Responsibilities**:
  * Direct Linear sprint planning, IDE automations, AI/LLM workshop curriculum, and DevHack 2027 hackathon architecture.
  * Oversee budget approvals, committee chairs, team leads, and internal governance.
  * Lead executive syncs and delegate external/statutory mandates.
* **Key KPIs**:
  * Delivery of premier workshops, hackathon execution, high engagement across CS/SE/EE/ME disciplines.

---

## 2. Vice President & Administrative Co-Leader (Asıl Başkan Yardımcısı) — Mahmut İhsan Avcı
* **Core Mission**: Serve as the President's executive right hand, managing administrative rigor, member intake, and operational compliance.
* **Direct Responsibilities**:
  * Coordinate member outreach (Stealth Mode intake for 10 founding members), WhatsApp community administration, and Google Form response ingestion.
  * Manage official documentation, meeting minutes, and event logistical bookings (Auditorium, A-Block, Labs).
* **Key KPIs**:
  * Full 10-member roster compliance, flawless administrative records.

---

## 3. Statutory / Formal President (SCC 2. Sınıf Resmi Temsilcisi) — Kaan Mete Şenyıldız
* **Core Mission**: Satisfy the university SCC statutory prerequisite requiring the Club President to be enrolled in at least the second year of study.
* **Direct Responsibilities**:
  * Serve as the formal signatory on official IUS Student Affairs (SKS/SCC) petitions and registration forms.
  * Represent the student body formally before the FENS Dean's Office, Academic Advisor, and university town halls in coordination with Hasan Kaan.
* **Key KPIs**:
  * Timely institutional signatures and maintaining official university club registration status.

## 4. Treasurer & Head of Sponsorships
* **Core Mission**: Secure financial sustainability, attract corporate sponsorships, and oversee budget discipline.
* **Direct Responsibilities**:
  * Draft the official Club Sponsorship Pitch Deck.
  * Pitch local tech companies (Sarajevo/Balkans/International) for event sponsorship and swag.
  * Maintain transparent financial records of revenues, donations, and expense receipts.
* **Key KPIs**:
  * Securing funding/in-kind support for catering, prizes, and hackathon swag.
  * 100% audited financial accounting reports.

---

## 5. Software & Artificial Intelligence Lead
* **Core Mission**: Build the technical credibility of the club through elite programming and AI content.
* **Direct Responsibilities**:
  * Organize hands-on coding sessions (e.g. Python for Data Science, Modern Web Dev, LLM agents).
  * Coordinate coding mentor pools for hackathons.
  * Maintain the club's open-source GitHub organization and digital projects.
* **Key KPIs**:
  * Delivery of at least 3 high-satisfaction technical workshops per semester.
  * Active student projects deployed and documented on GitHub.

---

## 6. Hands-on Engineering Projects Lead
* **Core Mission**: Facilitate multidisciplinary student project circles and practical semester prototypes across FENS.
* **Direct Responsibilities**:
  * Connect students interested in hardware, IoT, and embedded prototypes with lab access and components.
  * Coordinate lab access with FENS professors and technicians.
  * Organize peer review showcases and project demo days.
* **Key KPIs**:
  * Active student prototypes displayed during semester demo days.

---

## 7. PR, Branding & Social Media Lead
* **Core Mission**: Create a vibrant, unmistakable brand presence that attracts students, sponsors, and faculty.
* **Direct Responsibilities**:
  * Design modern, sleek promotional visuals (posters, banners, Instagram stories/reels, LinkedIn posts).
  * Run promotional campaigns across IUS campus and digital student groups.
  * Capture high-quality photos and video recaps during all events.
* **Key KPIs**:
  * Follower growth and engagement (>500 active followers in Semester 1).
  * Turnaround time for post-event recaps < 48 hours.
