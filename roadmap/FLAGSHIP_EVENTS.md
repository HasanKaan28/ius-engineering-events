# Flagship Event Blueprints

This document contains full operational concepts for the three flagship events planned for IUS Engineering Events Club.

---

## 1. Flagship Event: IUS DevHack 2026 (24-Hour Hackathon)

### Concept Overview
* **Description**: A high-intensity, 24-hour interdisciplinary hackathon bringing together students from Computer Science, Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Business to build functional prototypes solving real problems.
* **Duration**: 24 Hours (Saturday 09:00 to Sunday 15:00)
* **Venue**: IUS Main Campus (A-Block Amphitheater & FENS Labs)
* **Expected Attendees**: 60–80 students (15–20 teams of 3–4 members)

### Key Tracks / Themes
1. **AI & Intelligent Systems**: Autonomous agents, computer vision, NLP applications.
2. **Smart Campus & City**: IoT sensors, campus navigation, sustainability and energy tracking.
3. **Open Innovation**: High-impact problem solving using any modern engineering stack.

### Operational Requirements
* High-speed Wi-Fi assurance with IUS IT Center.
* Overnight security clearance with IUS Campus Security.
* Food & Refreshments: Coffee stations, midnight pizza, energy drinks, morning breakfast sponsored by local partners.
* Mentors & Judges: 3 corporate software architects + 2 FENS academic faculty members.

## 2. Flagship Event: Sarajevo Tech Horizon (Industry Summit & Networking Day)

### Concept Overview
* **Description**: An afternoon conference connecting IUS students with tech leaders, HR heads, and senior engineers from the Balkan tech ecosystem.
* **Duration**: 4–5 Hours
* **Venue**: IUS Main Auditorium
* **Format**:
  * **Keynote Speech**: "The State of Engineering & AI in Europe"
  * **Panel Discussion**: "What Top Tech Companies Actually Look For in Junior Engineers"
  * **Speed Networking Booths**: Dedicated sponsor tables where students submit CVs and discuss internship openings.
  * **Project Pitch Showcase**: 3 best student teams present their graduation/club projects to industry leaders.

---

## 3. Flagship Event: Engineering Showcase & Project Demo Day

### Concept Overview
* **Description**: An incubation and mentorship program designed to mentor, highlight, and connect multidisciplinary student engineering prototypes across FENS with local industry and faculty.
* **Format**:
  * Technical support and peer mentoring for student projects.
  * Access to lab workstations in FENS.
  * Bi-weekly milestone reviews with professors and senior developers.
  * Showcase presentation and demo booths in the IUS atrium.
